About Me

I am a final-year undergraduate student in a dual bachelor’s degree programme between BNBU (Beijing Normal University-Hong Kong Baptist University) and Hong Kong Baptist University (HKBU), major in Artificial Intelligence, Faculty of Science and Technology. My work sits where methods research meets systems that must survive contact with the physical world — from parameter-free frameworks that diagnose and suppress LLM bias, to segmentation networks for biomedical imaging, to generative models for drug discovery.

Research Interests

  • Trustworthy AI: LLM bias mitigation, causal attribution
  • AI for Science and Medicine: biomedical image segmentation, microscopy image restoration
  • Embodied AI: vision-language models for robotics, multi-agent navigation
